Clinical Background

The Diagnostic Challenge & The Eye-Tracking Biomarker

The Challenge
Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
  • ASD impacts 1 in 36 children globally, requiring urgent early diagnosis.
  • Current clinical methods (ADOS-2, ADI-R) are subjective and require certified clinicians.
  • Waiting lists often span 2–3 years, causing children to miss pivotal neuroplastic developmental windows.
The Modality
Why Eye-Tracking Scanpaths?
  • Human visual perception directly reflects social attentional and cognitive wiring.
  • Children with ASD display quantifiable differences in scanpaths when viewing social scenes.
  • Eye-tracking serves as an objective, rapid, and non-invasive digital screening modality.
Our Mission
Bridging Code to Clinical Practice
  • Achieve publication-grade accuracy on benchmark eye-tracking scanpaths.
  • Eliminate small-dataset overfitting using transfer representations and PCA.
  • Deliver an intuitive web interface with clear diagnostic reasoning.
Pipeline Architecture

4-Stage Research Pipeline (From Notebook to Model)

Stage 1
Preprocessing
  • Resize to (224, 224, 3)
  • Gaussian Blur (5x5, sigma=0) to eliminate line rendering artifacts
  • Min-Max normalization [0, 1]
Stage 2
MobileNet Features
  • Frozen ImageNet backbone
  • Pure feature extractor
  • Global Average Pooling
  • Outputs rich 1024-D spatial representations
Stage 3
PCA Manifold
  • Fitted PCA (N=250)
  • 1024-D compressed to 250-D
  • Captures >95.8% cumulative variance
  • Prevents overfitting
Stage 4
Stacking Ensemble
  • Base 1: Poly SVM (degree 4)
  • Base 2: KNN (k=3)
  • 5-Fold CV training
  • Meta: Logistic Regression

Architectural Insights
Why Stacking Outperformed Single Models
  • SVM Polynomial Projection captures complex non-linear boundary manifolds.
  • KNN Clustering models localized geometric sample density.
  • Logistic Regression Meta-Learner weights base learners to eliminate individual misclassifications.
  • MobileNet + PCA reduced inference latency by 82% compared to full-size ResNet models.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Oculomotor Biomarkers: Revealing the "Why"

BIOMARKER 1
Atypical Fixation Dispersion
Status: High Divergence

Fixations cluster tightly in peripheral or isolated zones rather than dispersing across holistic scene landmarks. Shows repetitive visual attention on non-salient elements.

BIOMARKER 2
Social-Salience Avoidance
Status: Reduced (31% vs 68% Norm)

Significant reduction in dwell time on primary visual social anchors (eyes, mouth, and human interaction cues), a canonical neurodevelopmental marker of ASD.

BIOMARKER 3
Saccadic Path Fragmentation
Status: Elevated Jitter & Tortuosity

Short, fragmented saccade jumps with high trajectory variability between fixations, indicating disrupted motor planning and visual scene exploration.

Cross-Domain Synergy

Extending Techniques to the Professor's ADHD Project

Handwriting Baseline Review
Current Limitations in ADHD Code
  • Vanilla CNN / ResNet: Achieves ~87% but uses naive 1D convolution heuristics for line straightness.
  • Loss of Kinematics: Treats handwriting as static pixels, ignoring velocity and pen pressure dynamics.
  • Small-Sample Overfitting: Deep CNNs with standard cross-entropy overfit on small pediatric cohorts.
Proposed Solutions
Advanced Algorithms to Implement
  • 1. Vision Transformers (Swin / ConvNeXt): Captures long-range page margin drift and slant.
  • 2. Supervised Contrastive Learning (SupCon): Builds robust clusters on small datasets without overfitting.
  • 3. Kinematic Reconstruction: Recovers temporal stroke velocity and jerk from paper scans.
  • 4. Multimodal Fusion: Combines Eye-Tracking + Handwriting for ASD vs ADHD differential diagnosis.
